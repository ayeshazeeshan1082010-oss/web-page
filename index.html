Our Story

Rooted in fire, guided by seasons.

Ember & Oak began as a backyard supper club in 2012 — twelve friends, one wood-fired grill, and a shared love of good food. Today we're a full restaurant, but that same spirit lives in everything we do.

Chef Marcus Rivera built our menu around a single idea: let the ingredients lead. Every dish starts with what's growing, what's fresh, and what's worth celebrating that week.

Locally Sourced

We partner with 14 farms and ranches within 50 miles of our kitchen.

Wood-Fired Cooking

Our custom oak-fired grill and oven are the heart of every plate we serve.
